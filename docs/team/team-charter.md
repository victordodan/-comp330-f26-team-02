## Communication

<!-- What communication channel(s) will you use?
     What response time should teammates normally expect?
     How will urgent issues be communicated? -->

We maintain communication via Microsoft Teams. When someone is mentioned in the chat (which sends a more urgent ping), the range of response time varies between immediate and a few hours later that day, either through the main chat or direct message. Urgent issues will be communicated also via Microsoft Teams, but with a mention of everyone in the group, which will send the urgent ping to everyone in the chat.

## Meetings and Coordination

<!-- How often will you meet?
     How will meetings be organized?
     How will decisions and action items be captured? -->

Brief meetings are scheduled in 2 groups throughout the week via Teams Meetings and Zoom Meetings. Not everyone is available at the same time, so we keep the meetings short since they happen twice, but especially when something important needs to be discussed, i.e. due dates/ expectations, we will extend our typical meeting time. Details are posted on the Teams group chat.

## Decision Making

<!-- How will normal engineering decisions be made?
     What happens when the team cannot reach agreement? -->

As a team with an even amount of people, we will run by the majority, but if we are evenly split, we will set up a meeting to discuss other courses of actions further.

## Participation and Accountability

<!-- What does the team consider reasonable participation?
     What should a member do if they cannot meet a commitment? -->

We consider participation as having the work done in a timely manner, whether it's a set due date from the professor or one we  assigned to ourselves. If a member cannot meet a commitment, we expect it be properly communicated to the team with enough time to adapt # and reorganize the workload. 

## Conflict and Escalation

<!-- How will the team address disagreements, missed commitments,
     or persistent participation problems?
     When should the instructor become involved? -->

As long as an internal conflict is not impeding on work completion and the overall process, it will be left to the 2 members to work  it amongst themselves, until it starts to impact the team, then there will be a briefing and conflict resolution course we will follow.  If there is a conflict between majority to another couple members, we will have to jump straight to conflict resolution via a meeting. If  those conflict resolution meetings don't work, the issue will be brought to the instructor. 

## AI and Engineering Responsibility

<!-- State your team's expectations for using AI-assisted engineering tools.
     Remember: the team remains responsible for understanding, verifying,
     testing, and defending its work. -->
     
AI-assisted work must follow the team's AI-Use Policy, and each member is responsible for reviewing and verifying AI-assisted output before committing their work and/ or submitting it. Significant AI assistance should be recorded in the team's AI-Use Log according to the agreed on policies, and any purely AI_generated conted will not be accepted without human review.

## Team Commitments

Beyond completion of work, each team member agrees to show decorum to other members, willingness to assist each other, and constant integrity in their character and performance of work.
